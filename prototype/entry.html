2 Row details

Merged cells in the PDF are entered on every row they cover

What changed — in the AAFA table the “Restriction / Maximum Limit” cell spans the Nonylphenol and the NPEO rows. Here each row carries its own copy of the text.

Why — a search for NPEO must return a complete row on its own. Copying the text is cheap; a merged-cell model makes search and export unreliable.

Several CAS numbers in one cell are entered one per line

What changed — OEKO-TEX lists six CAS numbers for Nonylphenol in one cell (“Various, 11066-49-2, …”). Type each on its own line; free text such as “Various” or “All CAS of isomers of NP” is allowed.

Why — the search matches every individual number, with or without dashes, and each number is checked against its check digit as you type.
