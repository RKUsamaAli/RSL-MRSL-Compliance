Substance list categories

One category per source document — each with its own column layout Search & data table + New category
How it fits together: a category is a document such as the AAFA RSL. You define its columns once (CAS Number, Chemical Name, Limit…). Then you add rows one by one under a section title taken from the document (e.g. “AP and APEO”). Search always works by CAS number and chemical name across every category, whatever the other columns are called.
